How to play
  • Everyone gets 5 cards. Blinds (starting 10/20) start the pot and rise every 5 hands. Start with 1000 chips.
  • Betting round 1: Fold, Check, Call, or Raise.
  • Draw: discard up to 3 cards (4 if you keep an Ace) and get new ones.
  • Betting round 2, then showdown. Best poker hand wins the pot (side pots for all-ins).
  • Last player with chips wins. Friends: open this same file, enter your room code.
